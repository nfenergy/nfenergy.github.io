Expertise

From initial sourcing

Selective commercial support at the stage when industrial and corporate opportunities are identified and introduced.

01 / NF ENERGY

Land origination

Direct sourcing of land and sites that may warrant preliminary consideration for solar PV, agrivoltaic and BESS projects, including initial information gathering against development criteria.

Technical, planning, environmental and permitting due diligence remains with qualified specialists.

02 / NF ENERGY

Projects and SPVs

Identification of potential project, shareholding and portfolio opportunities held by third parties, with initial collation of information and assessment against counterparty criteria.

Ownership, sale authority, permits and transaction documentation must be independently verified.

03 / NF ENERGY

Industrial matching

Targeted introductions to developers, IPPs, industrial groups and potential investors according to technology, location, scale, development stage and proposed deal structure.

An outreach or introduction alone does not evidence investment interest or an engagement.

04 / NF ENERGY

Commercial coordination

Support for initial dialogue and orderly disclosure of authorised information in relation to asset deals, share deals, partnerships and co-development discussions.

No regulated investment, reserved legal or tax advice, or licensable real estate brokerage is offered through this website.

Defining the scope of an engagement is part of delivering a professional service.

The quality of an opportunity begins with the quality of the information.
