# NF ENERGY — Energy & Infrastructure Advisory

Corporate website prototype, Italian and English. This is the public demonstration version, without third-party confidential project dossiers.

## Preview and publication

The root `index.html` is the Italian homepage and `en/index.html` is the English homepage. The `assets` directory contains photographs, CSS, JavaScript and corporate identity assets.

On GitHub Pages, publish the `main` branch from `/ (root)`. This repository must be under the corporate GitHub identity chosen by the company.

## Business and legal information

The logo reads **NF ENERGY**. The legal entity NF ENERGY S.R.L., registration information and office addresses appear separately in corporate disclosures.

This demonstration has **no active inquiry form**, no email or phone number, and no third-party project asset quantities. Prior to full launch, configure a company-controlled mailbox, replace the demonstration-only contact form, and approve the final privacy and legal information.

Creative images illustrate sectors and are not representations of NF ENERGY-owned assets.
